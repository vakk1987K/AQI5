1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

Location Data (Optional): When you click the "Locate Me" or GPS feature, the application requests access to your approximate or precise geographical coordinates (latitude and longitude). This data is strictly and solely used to locate the nearest air quality monitoring station and fetch ambient weather and rain forecasts for your immediate vicinity.

Ephemeral Processing Only: Your location coordinates are sent over encrypted connections to meteorological API endpoints in real-time. We do NOT store, log, record, or associate your location with any personal identity. Once the data is retrieved for your viewing session, your coordinates are discarded.

Zero Registration / No Personal Identity: The app does not require creating an account, signing in, or submitting personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, address book contacts, or financial details.

2. Third-Party Data Providers

To retrieve ambient atmospheric pollutant readings and precipitation estimates, the app connects to:

  • Open-Meteo API: Provides high-resolution global weather models and atmospheric composition data.

All data transmissions are secured using Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS) encryption to safeguard communications against interception.

3. Data Sharing, Selling, and Tracking

  • No Data Selling: We do NOT sell, lease, trade, or share your location data or device information with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks.
  • No Invasive Tracking SDKs: We do not embed behavioral tracking SDKs or ad profiling tools.
  • Data Retention: We store zero personal data on our servers.

4. User Rights and Permission Control

  • You can revoke location permissions at any time in your Android device settings: Settings → Apps → AQI-App → Permissions → Location → Don't allow.
  • The application remains completely functional without location permissions by simply searching or selecting any city manually.
  • Push and rain alert notifications can be toggled on or off directly inside the app or in device notification settings.
